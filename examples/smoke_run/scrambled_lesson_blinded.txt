LESSON: How an expert AI tells younger (under 40) from older (65+) adults on an ECG.
The AI relies on these measurements, most important first:
1. Measure 18: in older adults it tends to be lower (cut-point about 0.299; higher points to younger).
2. Measure 4: in older adults it tends to be higher (cut-point about 387; lower points to younger).
3. Measure 2: in older adults it tends to be higher (cut-point about 153; lower points to younger).
4. Measure 17: in older adults it tends to be lower (cut-point about 0.286; higher points to younger).
5. Measure 3: in older adults it tends to be lower (cut-point about 93.6; higher points to younger).
No single cue is decisive. Count how many cues point to 'older', giving more weight to the cues near the top of the list, and decide by the balance.
