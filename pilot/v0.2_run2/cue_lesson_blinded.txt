The AI relies on these measurements, most important first:
1. Measure 2: in older adults it tends to be higher (cut-point about 157; lower points to younger).
2. Measure 14: in older adults it tends to be lower (cut-point about 0.219; higher points to younger).
3. Measure 13: in older adults it tends to be higher (cut-point about -0.78; lower points to younger).
4. Measure 15: in older adults it tends to be higher (cut-point about 0.0025; lower points to younger).
5. Measure 11: in older adults it tends to be higher (cut-point about 0.402; lower points to younger).
6. Measure 7: in older adults it tends to be lower (cut-point about 33; higher points to younger).
7. Measure 17: in older adults it tends to be lower (cut-point about -0.02; higher points to younger).
8. Measure 5: in older adults it tends to be higher (cut-point about 428; lower points to younger).
No single cue is decisive. Count how many cues point to 'older', giving more weight to the cues near the top of the list, and decide by the balance.
