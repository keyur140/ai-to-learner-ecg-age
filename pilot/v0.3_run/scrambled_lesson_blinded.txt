LESSON: How an expert AI tells younger (under 40) from older (65+) adults on an ECG.
The AI relies on these measurements, most important first:
1. Measure 6: in older adults it tends to be lower (cut-point about 51; higher points to younger).
2. Measure 8: in older adults it tends to be higher (cut-point about 49; lower points to younger).
3. Measure 15: in older adults it tends to be lower (cut-point about 0; higher points to younger).
4. Measure 1: in older adults it tends to be lower (cut-point about 72; higher points to younger).
5. Measure 9: in older adults it tends to be lower (cut-point about 0.102; higher points to younger).
6. Measure 16: in older adults it tends to be lower (cut-point about 0.216; higher points to younger).
No single cue is decisive. Count how many cues point to 'older', giving more weight to the cues near the top of the list, and decide by the balance.
