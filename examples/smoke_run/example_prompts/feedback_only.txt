You are a student learning a new skill. You will see measurements taken from adults' resting 12-lead ECGs. Each ECG belongs either to a younger adult (under 40 years old) or to an older adult (65 years or older). Exactly half of all cases are younger and half are older.

LESSON: What the ECG measurements mean.
- Heart rate is the number of ventricular beats per minute.
- The PR interval is the time from the start of the P wave to the start of the QRS complex.
- QRS duration is how long the ventricles take to depolarise.
- The QT interval runs from the start of the QRS complex to the end of the T wave.
- QTc is the QT interval corrected for heart rate with Bazett's formula.
- The P-wave axis is the main direction of atrial depolarisation in the frontal plane.
- The QRS axis is the main direction of ventricular depolarisation in the frontal plane.
- The T-wave axis is the main direction of ventricular repolarisation in the frontal plane.
- P-wave height in lead II is the peak voltage of the atrial wave.
- P-wave duration in lead II is how long the atrial wave lasts.
